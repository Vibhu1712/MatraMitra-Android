Matra Mitra (मात्रा मित्र) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Matra Mitra is a free app that helps children learn Hindi letters and matras. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

No personal information is collected

The app does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not request internet access, so nothing is sent from the app to us or to anyone else.

What stays on the device

The app saves a few items only on the device itself, so it can remember them next time:

  • game scores for each matra (how many answers were right),
  • settings such as speaking speed, theme and English sound hints, and
  • the last letter and section that were open.

This information never leaves the device. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Progress → Reset inside the app, by clearing the app's storage in the phone settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Speech

To read letters aloud, the app uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on the phone (for example, Speech Services by Google). The app passes only the letter or word to be spoken to that engine. It does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Children

The app is designed for children and their parents or teachers. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
