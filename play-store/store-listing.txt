APP NAME (max 30 characters)
Matra Mitra: Hindi Matras

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 characters)
Learn every Hindi matra with sounds, words, tracing and fun games for kids.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
मात्रा मित्र (Matra Mitra) helps children learn to read Hindi, one letter and one matra at a time.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• वर्णमाला – all vowels and consonants, grouped the way school textbooks teach them, including क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ, श्र, ड़ and ढ़. Tap any letter to hear it with an example word.
• मात्राएँ – every matra: ा ि ी ु ू ृ े ै ो ौ ॉ ं ँ ः. See where each one sits on the letter, hear it, and find simple words that use it.
• छोटी–बड़ी जोड़ियाँ – practise the twins children mix up most: कि/की, कु/कू, के/कै, को/कौ.
• बारहखड़ी – pick any letter and see it with every matra, with example words. Play the whole row aloud.
• Stories – a short story for every consonant, with tappable coloured words.
• लिखो – trace letters with your finger and get up to three stars.
• खेलो – three games: hear and pick, read it, and finish the word with the right matra.
• Progress – see which matras are strong and which need practice, and play a round with just the weak ones.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Works fully offline.
• No ads, no sign-in, no data collected.
• Colour-coded matras: short sounds light, long sounds dark.
• English sound hints (ka, kaa, kay…) that can be switched off once your child reads the letters on their own.

Letters are read aloud by your phone's text-to-speech. If you hear nothing, install the Hindi voice: Settings → Text-to-speech → Speech Services by Google → Install voice data → Hindi (India). The app shows a shortcut when it's needed.
