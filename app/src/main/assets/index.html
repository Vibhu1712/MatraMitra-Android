छोटी–बड़ी जोड़ियाँ

These twins are the ones children mix up most. Tap each side and listen for the difference.

For the grown-up: Don't march through every letter in order. Start with the letters in the child's name and favourite words, add one new matra every few days, and keep circling back to whichever twin (कि/की, कु/कू, के/कै, को/कौ) trips them up. Ten focused minutes beats an hour of pressure.
